Maintenance Manager v5.9.9 - Separate Job Parts Ordered / Parts Used
=====================================================================

Changed from v5.9.8:
- public/app.js only

What changed
------------
The maintenance job editor now has two separate sections:

1. Parts ordered (optional)
   - Part
   - Part number
   - Quantity ordered
   - Unit price
   - Supplier
   - Date ordered

2. Parts used (optional)
   - Part
   - Part number
   - Quantity used
   - Unit price
   - Supplier
   - Date used / fitted

Example:
- Order 5 bearings at £50 each in Parts ordered.
- Fit/use 2 bearings in Parts used.
- The job keeps both records separately.
- Only the quantity in Parts used affects tracked stock.

Compatibility
-------------
Existing v5.9.6-v5.9.8 jobs that stored ordered and used quantities in the same part row are split automatically in the editor when opened. Saving the job stores the ordered and used records separately without losing the quantities.

Deployment
----------
Replace only:
  public/app.js

Then deploy normally through GitHub/Cloudflare.

No Cloudflare configuration changes are required.
No D1 migration is required.
