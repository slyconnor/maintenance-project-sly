Maintenance Manager v5.6.0 setup

1. Replace the changed GitHub files with the v5.6.0 files.
2. Let the existing Cloudflare Workers build/deploy complete.
3. No new D1 migration, environment variable, R2 bucket, Access policy, or Worker binding is required for stock control.
4. Open Parts -> Parts Stock Control.
5. Press Edit stock beside a part, enable Track stock, enter the physical Current Stock, Minimum Stock, and optional Bin / Location, then save.
6. Add that part to a maintenance job and save the job. The Current Stock will reduce by the job quantity.
7. Current Stock <= Minimum Stock is shown as Low Stock. Current Stock <= 0 is shown as Out of Stock and is also counted as low.

Important: when tracking is first enabled for a part, the current quantity is an as-of-now physical count. Old historical jobs are not deducted from it again.
