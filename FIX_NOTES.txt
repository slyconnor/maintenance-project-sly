Maintenance Manager v5.6.0

New in this version
- Live parts stock control replaces the placeholder.
- Any saved part can be marked as stock-tracked with Current Stock, Minimum Stock and Bin / Location.
- Adding a tracked part to a job automatically deducts the used quantity when the job is saved.
- Editing job part quantities reconciles the stock difference instead of deducting twice.
- Removing a stock-tracked part from a saved job restores the quantity that had actually been deducted.
- Deleting a job restores only stock quantities that were deducted by the stock-control system.
- Low stock is highlighted when Current Stock is less than or equal to Minimum Stock.
- Out-of-stock parts are highlighted separately.
- Existing historical jobs are not retroactively deducted when stock tracking is first enabled; the Current Stock number you enter is treated as the known physical count now.
- Job part pickers show the current known stock and LOW warning.

No new Cloudflare bindings or D1 migration are required for v5.6.0. Stock fields are stored in the existing shared app state.
