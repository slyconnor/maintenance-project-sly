Maintenance Manager v5.9.10 - Remember last purchase price

CHANGED FROM v5.9.9
- public/app.js only

WHAT CHANGED
- When a part is selected on a new Open Order, the unit price is automatically filled from the most recent PLACED order for that part.
- If the currently selected supplier has previously supplied that part, its most recent price is preferred.
- If there is no history for that supplier, the most recent placed-order price from any supplier is used.
- The remembered price is only a starting value. It remains fully editable before the order is placed.
- Existing saved/ordered records are not changed.

DEPLOYMENT
Replace public/app.js and deploy normally.
No D1 migration and no Cloudflare configuration changes are required.
